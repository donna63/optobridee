Optob Ride: Empowering the future of rides and deliveries - swift, smart, unstoppable

At Optob Ride, movement isn't just what we do—it's who we are. It drives us to innovate, to reimagine mobility, and to deliver at the speed of now. From people to packages, we're building the future of how the world moves—smarter, faster, everywhere.

A letter from our
CEOs

Movement inspires us. It gets us going every morning and keeps us striving to do better. With Optob Ride, every journey matters—whether you're catching a ride, sending a package, or earning on the go. We're here to make moving simple, reliable, and future-ready.

Mission

  • • To provide efficient and reliable transportation and services service to clients, optimizing supply chain and building strong customers relationship.
  • • To connect people, products and service providers all around the world.
  • • Optob Ride exists to revolutionize mobility and logistics for a world that demands speed, efficiency, and sustainability.
  • • Our mission is to connect people and goods in real time through innovative, future-ready solutions that are safe, seamless, and environmentally conscious.
  • • We aim to empower drivers with tools to earn fairly, couriers with opportunities to deliver faster, and users with reliable access to movement that adapts to their lifestyle.
  • • Together, we are building more connected cities, more opportunities for individuals, and more pathways to a cleaner, smarter future

Our Core Services

Our core transportation services is based on motorcycle, Car, mini van conveying of product/produce from company/farmland, construction site to their desired destination and on the long run we venture into air and rail transport and extensively thereby creating a supply chain where of building materials such as granite, sand, cement can be ordered through Optop rides.

Your safety drives us

Your safety is the foundation of everything we build. With every ride and every delivery, Optob Ride integrates cutting-edge technology, real-time monitoring, and smart systems designed to protect you—whether you're in the back seat, behind the wheel, or on the move with a parcel. Together with safety partners and advocates, we're shaping a future where mobility is not just fast and efficient, but secure for all
